If a death has just occurred

You don’t have to work out the next steps alone. Call us and we will walk you through them.

  1. Call the right people first

How it works

From the first phone call to returning your loved one’s remains, here is what happens.

  1. Call us

    Call (215) 487-9022 or send the form below. We answer your questions and explain both options.

  2. Your loved one is brought into care

    Our partner funeral home brings your loved one into their care and starts the paperwork.

  3. Plan the service

    Meet with Rev. Harris to choose the date, readings, music, speakers and the obituary.

  4. Paperwork is filed

    The funeral home files the death certificate with Pennsylvania and orders your copies.

  5. Gather and remember

    A one-hour visitation, then the service, with flowers, music and memorial cards.

  6. Remains returned

    After cremation, your loved one’s remains are returned to your family.

Funeral service FAQs

Do we have to be members of Grace Community Church?

No. We serve any family in Philadelphia and the surrounding area who needs help, whether or not they attend Grace.

What is the difference between a memorial service and a funeral?

At a funeral, your loved one is present in a casket and there is usually a viewing. At a memorial service, cremation happens first, so there is no viewing. Both bring family and friends together to remember and pray.

My loved one did not want a viewing. Can we still have a service?

Yes. A memorial service with cremation lets family and friends pay their respects and support one another while honoring your loved one’s wishes.

Where is the service held?

In a church, Monday through Saturday, or at another church or facility you choose. Another venue’s fees are extra, and we will travel to meet your family’s needs.

What is not included in the price?

A repast is $500 extra. Fees for another church or facility, and extra costs when a death occurs at a nursing home, hospice or residence, are quoted before you agree to anything.

Who handles the death certificate?

Our partner funeral home files the death certificate with the state of Pennsylvania and obtains the copies you will need to handle your loved one’s affairs.
