About Grace Community Church

Who we are and what we believe.

Grace Community Church is a church in Philadelphia built on three commitments.

  • WeLove God
  • WeLove people
  • WeDo good

Words can only capture a fraction of what makes our church family so special, but at our core, we are a community rooted in faith, grace, and purpose. Built on a strong biblical foundation, we are driven to live out the gospel every day.

We are a vibrant, multi-generational church with a deep heart for outreach, dedicated to bringing hope, light, and real support to our surrounding neighborhood and beyond. We offer a welcoming space to cultivate genuine Christian community—a place where people of all ages can build meaningful relationships, encourage one another, and belong.

Through every season of life, we maintain an unyielding commitment to spiritual growth, nurturing disciples and following the Holy Spirit’s guidance. Blessed by God’s daily grace, we walk together in unity, love, and joy, striving to make a lasting, positive difference in our church and in the world around us.

We believe in God the Father Almighty, maker of heaven and earth. We believe in Jesus Christ, His only Son and our Lord, who was crucified, rose again on the third day and will return. We believe in the Holy Spirit, in one universal Church, in the forgiveness of sins, in the resurrection of the body and in life everlasting.

Drawn from the Apostles’ Creed.

Dear Friend,

On behalf of the Grace Community Church family, welcome to our digital home! We are so glad you have taken the time to visit our website and discover what we are all about.

We are standing at the threshold of a dynamic, exciting new season. I believe God is leading us onto a fresh and uncharted path filled with energy, innovation, and purpose. We are stepping boldly into new territory together, expectant for all the ways God will move in our lives and in our surrounding community. As we launch into this new chapter, please know that you aren’t just a site visitor—you are warmly invited to step into this adventure with us.

A scripture that continues to inspire our vision for this journey comes from the book of Numbers, where Moses invites his family to join them on the trail to the promised land:

“We’re leaving for the place the Lord has promised us. He has said that all will go well for us. So come along, and we will make sure that all goes well for you.” (Numbers 10:29 CEV)

Just like Moses, we want to invite you to “come along” and help shape what’s next. Whether you are exploring faith for the very first time, looking for a place to belong, or ready to deepen your walk with Jesus, there is a place for you here at Grace Community Church.

Feel free to connect with us if you have any questions or if we can support you in any way. We can’t wait to meet you in person soon!

Always with love (and forever in His service),
Rev. Arian B. Harris
