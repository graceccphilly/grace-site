Car donation questions

Don’t see yours? Call (215) 487-9022 or email info@gracecommunityphilly.org.

What kinds of vehicles do you accept?

Cars, SUVs, pickup trucks, vans, minivans, buses and semi trucks, in any condition, running or not.

Does it have to run?

No. We accept vehicles whether they run or not.

Do you pick up outside Philadelphia?

Yes. Our free towing covers every city and town within 60 miles of South Philadelphia, including the Philadelphia suburbs, South and Central Jersey, northern Delaware and Cecil and Harford counties in Maryland. See areas we serve.

Is towing really free?

Yes. Our towing partner picks up your vehicle at no cost to you, running or not.

How does the vehicle get to you?

Our towing partner picks it up for free, wherever it’s parked. When we call, we’ll set a time that works for you, and someone from Grace will meet you and the tow truck there. Have the keys ready, and your notarized title if you have one.

How soon will you call?

Within two business days of receiving your details.

How do I sign over the title?

Sign it in front of a notary before pickup. Most tag and notary shops in Philadelphia do it in a few minutes for a small fee. Bring your photo ID, sign only where we show you on the call, and leave the rest blank. Then put the title in the glove compartment or hand it to us at pickup.

Can I donate a car without a title?

Yes. No title? No problem. Choose “No, or not sure” on the form, and we’ll take care of the paperwork with you. Your registration or insurance card can help show that the vehicle is yours.

What do I do with my license plate?

Take it off before pickup. The plate belongs to you, not the vehicle. You can move it to your next vehicle; if you won’t use it, return it to your state’s motor vehicle agency (in Pennsylvania, PennDOT’s Return Tag Unit). We’ll go over the details on the call.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Grace Community Church is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so your vehicle donation can be deducted if you itemize on your federal tax return.

What you’ll get from us

  • A written receipt after pickup, with the vehicle’s year, make, model and VIN.
  • If the vehicle is worth more than $500: IRS Form 1098-C, sent within 30 days of the sale (or of the donation, if Grace keeps and uses the vehicle).

How much you can deduct

  • If Grace sells the vehicle: usually the amount it sold for.
  • If Grace uses the vehicle in its work: usually its fair market value.
  • If it sells for $500 or less: its fair market value, up to $500.

Every situation is different, so check with your tax advisor. IRS Publication 4303, A Donor’s Guide to Vehicle Donation, explains the rules in plain language.
