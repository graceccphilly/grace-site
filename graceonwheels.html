Project: Grace on Wheels

Donate a car, truck or van.

Put a vehicle you no longer need to work for the ministry and our South Philly neighbors.

Tell us about your vehicle How it works

Vehicle donations

Your old vehicle, a new ministry

Grace Community Church is a church on the move. A donated vehicle helps us carry food to families, bring neighbors to worship and get volunteers where they are needed.

Tell us about you and the vehicle in a short form, and someone from Grace will call you about next steps.

We accept

Cars, station wagons, SUVs, pickup trucks, vans and buses.

Running or not

Tell us the condition in the form, and we’ll let you know if we can take it.

Your title

Have it ready if you can. Not sure where it is? Tell us, and we’ll talk it through.

How it works

Four steps from your driveway to the ministry.

  1. Tell us about it

    Fill in the short form below with your details and the vehicle’s.

  2. We call you

    Someone from Grace calls to talk through the vehicle and answer your questions.

  3. Hand it over

    We agree on a day to sign over the title and pick up or drop off the vehicle.

  4. Get your receipt

    We send a written receipt for your records.
